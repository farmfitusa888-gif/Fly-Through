Ninety-five per cent of what you pay is not the render.

## So what is it?

Three things, and they are the whole business.

**Judgement about the order.** Photographs arrive as a folder. A film needs a
sequence — a buyer's walk, not a file listing. Which room opens, which room
closes, where the camera goes outside and comes back in. Get it wrong and you
have a slideshow with motion.

**Refusing the shots that will not work.** A rear-facing patio shot cut to a
front-framed aerial produces a film that looks like a different house, because
there is no path a camera could fly between them. We check for that before
rendering and tell you, which occasionally means telling a paying customer that
their photo set needs one more picture.

**The disclosure pack.** An on-screen mark, a hosted page carrying your
unaltered originals, a QR code, and caption text. That page is the artefact
California's §10140.8 requires a link to, and building it properly is most of
the reason this is a business rather than a script.

## Why the market prices are what they are

A traditional listing video runs several hundred dollars, and a drone package
not much less, because
somebody drives to the property. That is a real cost and those prices are
honest. What they buy is a person with a camera and an afternoon.

We are not cheaper because we found a cheaper way to do that. We are cheaper
because **we do not do it at all** — we start from photographs that already
exist. It is a different product that happens to sit in the same slot on an
invoice.

## What this tells you about a suspiciously cheap quote

If somebody quotes you thirty dollars for AI listing video, the render is not what they are
saving on. At these costs nobody is saving meaningfully on the render.

They are saving on the judgement and the disclosure pack — which is to say, they
are shipping you the file and keeping none of the liability. That is a real
thirty-dollar product. It is just not the same product, and the difference shows up when
a buyer asks why the back of the house looks like that.

## And about an expensive one

Equally: a four-figure quote for AI video from your own photos is charging you
agency rates for a process that has no crew, no travel and no shoot day. Ask
what the extra thousand buys. There may be a good answer — a real editor, a
custom cut per platform, a strategist who knows your market. There may not be.

## The number we would rather you took away
