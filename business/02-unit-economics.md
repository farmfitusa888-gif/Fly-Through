**These figures do not deduct a founder salary.** Profit here *is* founder

contractor's rate from the $/hr column.

### What the table actually says

- **Effective hourly stays near $440 at every scale.** This business does not get
  more profitable per hour as it grows — it gets *bigger*. Growth comes from more
  hours or fewer minutes per job, not from operating leverage on cost.
- **Costs never exceed 6.4% of revenue** in any scenario. Compute is not a lever
  worth optimising. Ignore it.
- **Watch the recurring column.** At 46% recurring, half of next month's revenue
  already exists before any outreach. Below ~30%, this is freelancing with extra
  steps.

## 3. The real constraint

From §1 and the sensitivity analysis: credits would have to cost **43× more** to
erase gross margin, and the business survives a **5× credit price error** at 85.9%
margin.

> **Compute is not the constraint. Operator minutes are.**

Three operating rules follow, and they are counter-intuitive enough to be worth
stating explicitly:

1. **Spend credits to save minutes, always.** Render two variants of a risky shot
   in parallel ($0.70) rather than inspect one and re-roll it serially (10
   minutes). This trade is favourable by roughly 100×.
2. **Never downgrade quality to save render cost.** 720p saves $1.57 per job and
   costs quality on the only artefact the client judges. Never take this trade.
3. **Automate review, not rendering.** The single highest-value engineering task
   in the whole business is automated drift detection at operations step 7,
   because it attacks the only scarce input.

The one materially unverified input is the credit price ($0.0030, see

| Case | $/credit | Render/job | Cash GP | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as modelled | 0.0030 | $5.51 | $235.91 | 94.7% |
| 2× | 0.0060 | $11.03 | $230.40 | 92.5% |
| 3× | 0.0090 | $16.54 | $224.89 | 90.3% |
| 5× | 0.0150 | $27.57 | $213.86 | 85.9% |

A five-fold pricing error moves gross margin by 8.8 points. **The business
conclusion does not change under any plausible error**, which is the only reason
it is acceptable to proceed on an unverified input — and it must still be
verified before Phase 2.
